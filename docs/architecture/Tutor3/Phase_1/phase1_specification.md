# Purpose

This specification governs Tutor 3 Phase 1.

Tutor 3 Phase 1 translates validated business semantics into an executive information architecture.

Its purpose is to determine **what information deserves attention, in what order, and why**.

# Information Design Principles

## Principle 1

Business questions drive visual hierarchy.

---

## Principle 2

Information importance determines placement.

---

## Principle 3

Visual encoding follows business meaning.

---

## Principle 4

Cognitive load should be minimized.

---

## Principle 5

Executive attention should naturally flow from highest-impact information to supporting evidence.

---

## Principle 6

No visual recommendation may contradict analytical or semantic truth.

# Validation Questions

Every information group must answer:

```
What business question does this answer?

Why is it important?

Where should executive attention begin?

Which semantic objects support it?

Which future financial metrics will occupy this area?

How does this reduce cognitive effort?
```

---

# Explicit Non-Goals

Tutor 3 Phase 1 must not perform:

- Wireframes
- KPI creation
- Financial calculations
- SQL
- Data modeling
- Semantic redesign
